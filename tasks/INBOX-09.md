## Goal

Creators who leave too many paid questions unanswered are warned, then paused for 7 days (R-13). Additive only.

## Scope

Rolling 30-day unanswered rate, warning and switch-off per R-13, enforced when sending and shown on the profile.

## Capability checks

- [ ] A creator at 21% unanswered over 30 days is warned once; at 31% paid questions switch off for 7 days and a fan sees I8. The thresholds (20% and 30%) come from config.

**Findings**

1. **defect** (latent scale, low): the sweep never reaches creators beyond the first 500. `PaidDmPauseService.sweep()` takes `distinct creatorWawuId` with `take: 500` and no `orderBy`, and the flagged list is also `take: 500`. Creators already paused or warned keep their misses in the window, so the same 500 fill the list on every run. With 620 creators each holding one lapsed question, three consecutive runs each reached 500; 500 were paused and notified, 120 never were (my `SWEEP-SCALE` probe). Send-time enforcement still works for those 120 (it evaluates on every send), but their warning and pause notification and their write-back at the end of a pause never happen from the sweep, which is the sweep's whole job ("without anyone looking"). Fix by paging through all candidates (cursor on `creatorWawuId`) or ordering by least recently checked.
2. **unclear** (owner, G-77, already filed, `PROVISIONAL` present): R-13 says only "warned at 20% unanswered paid questions and paid messages pause for 7 days at 30%". It names no minimum count and no basis, so the default minimum of 1 is the builder's literal reading: a creator's first lapsed question reads 100% and pauses paid messages for 7 days (my `MIN-1` test: 1 missed gives `paused`, 100%). Nothing in R-13 or the task text decides otherwise, so it is not a defect, but it is a harsh outcome the owner should settle before launch. Same entry covers the basis (share of questions sent in the last 30 days whose outcome is known: answered, or past their deadline; questions still inside their window count for nothing yet) and "at or over" (the canvas copy says "over 20%" and "Past 30%"; R-13 says "at", R-13 outranks the canvas; only the exact line differs). All three are as G-77 says; I checked each against the code and boundaries.
3. **differs-for-recorded-reason**: `GET /dm/response-stats` (live route, web and dashboard read it) now reports `penaltyState` `warned` or `disabled_7d`, the stored percentage and `dmDisabledUntil` for a creator at or over a line, once the standing has been evaluated (a send attempt, either new read, or the sweep). 4. **unclear** (task text "shown on the profile"): the profile routes the web calls (`GET /users/:id/public-profile`, professional routes) are unchanged for everyone, so they still say a paused creator takes paid messages (identical on main and branch in my scenarios, G-78). 5. **unclear** (minor, no user effect): the builder's "the tracker row is only written when the standing moves" is slightly off. Also `GET availability` by any signed-in user can cause that write and the creator's notification, harmless.
6. **unclear** (low): the notification is written after the transaction commits and `NotificationService.emit` swallows errors. Under concurrency it is exactly once per transition (proved above), but if that one insert fails, the state is already stored (`warned` or paused) and nothing retries it, so that transition has no notification. The builder's design decision; worth a line in G-77 or accepting.
7. **unclear** (not this task's, mobile): `OpenWalletScreen.test.tsx` "a user sees a loading view first..." times out at 5 s with a cold jest cache (`npx jest --clearCache` then `npx jest src/features/open-wallet` fails 1 of 54 every time; warm runs pass).
