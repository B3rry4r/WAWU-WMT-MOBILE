### Findings (all low; none blocks merge)
- **L1 · defect (low) · consent time is the Hub's first record, not the WAWU ID sign-up.** `src/movies/movies.service.ts:311` (`agreementFor`). Repro: person registered in WAWU ID through /join (or started sign-up) before `FLIXORA_TERMS_FROM`, first finishes Hub onboarding after it: profile `createdAt` >= date, so `mustAgreeTerms` is false and the link is given without MV4's box. Affected: people registered through /join before the date who first open the app after it, and anyone mid sign-up across the date. Fix (not possible inside the Hub): WAWU ID adds a sign-up time claim to `buildAccessPayload` (or to `/internal/users/lookup`); the Hub then reads it in `agreementFor` only. Until then the owner can leave `FLIXORA_TERMS_FROM` empty (everyone sees the box) or accept this group. Also the `signup` consent row's `agreedAt` is the first tap, not the sign-up time (`movies.service.ts:230`).
- **L2 · defect (low) · empty catalog after a deploy.** `src/movies/movie-sync.service.ts:79`: only `@Cron('*/5 * * * *')` runs the sync, no pass at boot. Repro: start the Hub with `MOVIE_PARTNER=standin`; `GET /movies` is `enabled:true` with `rails:[]` until the next 5-minute mark (seen: 0 rows, then 14 at the tick). FLIX-03 must draw the empty state; a boot-time first pass would remove the gap.
- **L3 · defect (low) · a title can be dropped by a full pass on a shifting catalog.** `movie-sync.service.ts:225`: the full pass deletes rows not seen in that pass. If the partner unpublishes a title while the pages are being read, a title at a page boundary can be skipped and deleted; the incremental pass restores it only if it changed, so it can stay missing until the next full pass (24 h). Rare, self-healing.
- **L4 · note (low) · FLIX-02 does need one line outside `src/flixora/`.** Registering the adapter is `flixora: () => ...` in `src/movie-partner/movie-partner.module.ts` (same as `wallet-provider.module.ts` for R-39) and its settings in `.env.example`; nothing else (the interface already holds `updatedSince`, the movie path and the person fields; `region` is the adapter's own setting).
