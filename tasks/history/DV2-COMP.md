- **1 (medium) the web measuring loop:** reproduced on the verifier's export (`line` 123,456,789 in 150 px: 90 commits in 1.5 s, the count flipping 13.92 and 14.065 px). Two causes, both fixed. (a) React Native Web's `onLayout` reports `offsetWidth`, whole pixels; a whole-pixel width divided by a scale of 0.96 is out by up to a pixel, so the estimate of the full width differed by about a pixel between two scales, and the scale (in steps of 0.01) flipped. The width is now read to a fraction of a pixel from the event's element (`getComputedStyle`), falling back to the event's width where there is no DOM. (b) The drawn scale was kept in a passive effect: now a layout effect, set in the commit that draws it. The shape changed too: the count and the word are drawn at their natural width (`flexShrink 0`), each reports its own width, the box is as wide as the figure at full size, and the scale and layout are a pure function of the room and those two full widths (`pointsFitLayout`), so nothing the figure draws feeds back into the room. A re-measure under 0.5 px is ignored; after `pointsRemeasureCap` (8) accepted changes for one count in one room the figure keeps what it has. ## Builder report, round 4

Files changed: `src/components/PointsFigure.tsx` (the fitting only), `src/app/preview/points-figure.tsx` (cases added: 15 digits, and the Studio chip at 9, 12 and 15 digits in 120 and 150 px), `src/__tests__/dv2-comp.test.tsx` (89 tests became 97), this file and `tasks/DV2-COMP.md`. The lead's ruling: no measure-and-rescale feedback loop at all (rounds 2 and 3 each closed one oscillation and opened another; the round 3 verifier's C++ port of the loop on React Native 0.86.3's own Yoga found 192 of 3888 phone cases that never settle, finding 1).

- **The design as built.** Three numbers that never depend on how the figure is drawn: (a) the ROOM, the `onLayout` width of the figure's outer box, which is `width: 100%` of its parent (a row may shrink it), so whatever is drawn inside it cannot change it; (b) and (c) the NATURAL width of the count and of the word at the board's size, read once per (text, size) from invisible copies: absolute children of a clipped 0 by 0 row box, `opacity` 0, hidden from screen readers, never scaled, re-mounted (`key`) for a new text so a new text of the same width still reports. `pointsFit` is a pure function of those three: full size where `star + count + word + 2 px` fit; else one line at the largest share of full size, the count held at 11 px and the word at 10 px at the least (`pointsReadableFloor`, each its own floor); else the word under the count with the count worked out against the room beside the star alone, again at 11 px at the least; else (no word to move: the Studio chip, the Points tile; or the count is still wider than its room at 11 px) the count is drawn at 11 px and breaks only between its digit groups, whole groups as a wrapping row (`pointsCountGroups`: "123,", "456," ...), never inside a group, never an ellipsis. The 2 px (`pointsFitAllowance`) covers the whole-pixel readings of the web and the layout grid of a phone (text is rounded up to it): it is taken off the room only where the figure has to shrink. There is no tolerance, no cap, no read-back of what was drawn and no `getComputedStyle`: the web and the phone take the same path (no `adjustsFontSizeToFit`). Re-computed only when the room, the count or the size changes; one render after the three are known. Removed: `pointsFitScale`, `pointsFullWidth`, `pointsFitLayout`, `pointsFitMinScale`, `pointsStackMinScale`, `pointsRemeasureCap`, `remeasureTolerance`, the drawn-scale refs and `eventWidth`.
- **First paint (round 3 low 2).** Until the room and both natural widths for the current text are known, the visible figure is drawn at full size at `opacity` 0, so it keeps its height and nothing below jumps, and nothing is seen past the parent. A new count hides it again until the new count is measured (a word that did not change keeps its width).
- **One thing the web taught.** On the web `numberOfLines` adds `max-width: 100%`, which is nothing inside the copies' 0 px box, so the copies carry none (a count or a word has no place to wrap). Found by running the export, not by the model.
