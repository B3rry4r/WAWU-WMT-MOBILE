## Builder report, round 3

Files changed: `src/components/PointsFigure.tsx`, `VoicePlayer.tsx`; `src/app/preview/points-figure.tsx` (the fit cases now 7, 9 and 12 digits in 120, 150, 182 and 200 px); `src/__tests__/dv2-comp.test.tsx` (60 tests became 89); this file and `tasks/DV2-COMP.md`.

- **1 (medium) the web measuring loop:** reproduced on the verifier's export (`line` 123,456,789 in 150 px: 90 commits in 1.5 s, the count flipping 13.92 and 14.065 px). Two causes, both fixed. (a) React Native Web's `onLayout` reports `offsetWidth`, whole pixels; a whole-pixel width divided by a scale of 0.96 is out by up to a pixel, so the estimate of the full width differed by about a pixel between two scales, and the scale (in steps of 0.01) flipped. The width is now read to a fraction of a pixel from the event's element (`getComputedStyle`), falling back to the event's width where there is no DOM. (b) The drawn scale was kept in a passive effect: now a layout effect, set in the commit that draws it. The shape changed too: the count and the word are drawn at their natural width (`flexShrink 0`), each reports its own width, the box is as wide as the figure at full size, and the scale and layout are a pure function of the room and those two full widths (`pointsFitLayout`), so nothing the figure draws feeds back into the room. A re-measure under 0.5 px is ignored; after `pointsRemeasureCap` (8) accepted changes for one count in one room the figure keeps what it has. Evidence, dev export (own TMPDIR, only localhost:7620 and 7627 baked), the verifier's sweep with a stricter test (any commit in the 250 ms after a 150 ms settle): `hero`, `row` and `line` x 12,500, 1,250,000, 123,456,789 and 123,456,789,012, widths 100 to 260 step 2 plus 101, 107, 109, 123, 127, 129, 149, 151: 0 idle commits at 1068 of 1068 width changes, at most 1 commit per width change, nothing more than 0.6 px past the parent. The five named repros (and the 12 digit ones) settle with 0 commits idle. Tests: a stand-in layout engine (digits 0.6 em, commas 0.3, the word 3 em; each event read from the screen as it is when delivered, as the web does) over the same widths, 8 size and count pairs with exact widths (at most 4 commits per width change, nothing past the room) and 5 with whole-pixel widths (within the cap plus 4); and a test that 40 alternating widths stop changing the figure. With the 0.5 px tolerance and the cap removed, all five whole-pixel tests fail (checked, then restored).
- **2 (low, the lead's ruling) a number is never cut:** where one line cannot fit at the floor (0.5), the word "points" moves under the count in the same style and the count takes the room beside the star at the largest scale that fits (never an ellipsis; `cut: false` on every measured count); with no word to move (the Studio chip, the Points tile) the count goes below the floor, to `pointsStackMinScale` 0.25, at the least.
