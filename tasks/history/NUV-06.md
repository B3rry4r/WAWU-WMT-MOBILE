**Decisions (Default (agent), owner may override)**
1. "Leave the figures unconfirmed (`figuresStand` false)" is built as: nothing is written from a record that states no charge. With `figuresStand` false and no charge the ledger would compare a fee of 0 with the sending feature's quote and write a false "feeKobo 6500 vs 0" disagreement, and a row the handler creates would still carry fee 0, which D1b forbids. So a completed or reversed record without a charge waits (the row stays as the sender left it, `providerFeeKobo` untouched); when the charge is stated `figuresStand` stays true and the charge is taken as in decision 2.
2. A stated charge is compared with the row's `providerFeeKobo`, not `feeKobo` (which holds WAWU's fee on top, R-10). A row with no `providerFeeKobo` simply takes Nuvion's. The row completes (it is not held), keeps the quoted `feeKobo` and `totalKobo`, and carries the note; MONEY-16 and NUV-08 read it. WALLET-09 must write `providerFeeKobo` with its row (BACKEND_GAPS G-652 f).
3. A transfer answer in `failed` is read as a failed send only when it is this very send (the same checks as a receipt); a `failed` answer for another amount, account or payee stays `duplicate_reference`. A failed send's record is not charged: no note and no provider charge are written for it.
4. The N1 test is strict on all three fields (`payment_type` is `bank-transfer`, `type` is `outflow`, currency `NGN`): a list row that lacks one is `unknown`, never `found` and never `absent`.
5. N8: only a stated rail other than `bank-transfer` is left alone; a refund credit that states no rail stays a stop (it may be a bank send's).
