### The builder's decisions, ruled (Default (verifier), the lead and owner may override)

1. **The sweep is not inside the payment: accepted.** The payment code is untouched, the sweep reads the finished state exactly (completed, `openKey` given up, no review mark), and a delivery or a charge in review keeps the share with it. It lands within about a minute (11 s and 49 s in my runs) as long as the cron runs; it needs `ScheduleModule` loaded as the other sweeps do. Wrong only if a feature must not be paid until its sale settles differently: no.
2. **One move per earning, no batching: accepted for launch.** It keeps `wawu-earn-<payment id>` and one lease per sale, which is what makes "once" provable. The cost is G-703.
3. **`earned` is the payee's `payeeShareKobo`; held kinds count on release: accepted.** Confirmed with a release, a refund and a creator with no wallet (`s5`): a refund is never earned, a released hold is earned at once and landed when its move is confirmed, and no payment is counted twice.

5. **The month is the Lagos month of completion: accepted.** Midnight checked at both edges and across the year end, equal to ME-10's `lagosMonthOf`; F2 names the one boundary test that is missing.
6. **A share under a kobo is no earning: accepted.** It happens for a price of 1 kobo only; the kobo stays WAWU's.
7. **`waiting` counts everything earned and not confirmed, review included: accepted.** A reviewed earning reads as waiting until a person acts, so W39 should not promise a time.
8. **The provider's charge on the move is WAWU's (R-31): accepted as the recorded default.**
9. **Nuvion `not_supported` waits with a loud log: accepted;** it cannot arise on main today (NUV-05 has the book transfer).
