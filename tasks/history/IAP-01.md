Findings:
1. **high, defect** `src/store/store-purchase.service.ts:155` and `src/store/store-notification.service.ts:293`: a person cannot take a transaction already granted to someone else, but they can take a RENEWAL of someone else's subscription. Repro (probe P3 and P3b): A buys tier subscription token T, order `..0`; the store renews to order `..1` (the Hub has not heard); B posts T to `POST /store/purchases`: 201, B holds the tier; A posts T: 409 `store_purchase_taken`; the next RENEWED notice (order `..2`) is granted to B (rows: `..0` A, `..1` B, `..2` B). Same on iOS with a later `transactionId` of the same `originalTransactionId`: 201 for B. An Apple transaction id is not a secret. The brief's check "other-person token grants nothing" fails. Fix: in `settle` refuse when any `StorePurchase` with the same `(platform, subscriptionKey)` belongs to another person; and bind a first purchase to the person with Google `obfuscatedExternalAccountId` and Apple `appAccountToken`, read from the store's answer (nothing reads them today), so a token alone is not enough.
2. low, defect `src/store/clients/google-play.client.ts:86`: `verifyOneTime` returns the product id it was asked for, never the `productId` Google's answer carries, so the "that purchase is for another product" check in `buy` cannot fire for a Google pack and rests on Google rejecting a mismatched SKU. Compare `body.productId` when present.
3. low, defect `src/store/store-grant.service.ts` (`throw new Error(...)` in `give`): a paid purchase whose product points at a pack or tier that has left the plan answers a bare 500 (probe P4). Nothing is lost (nothing is consumed, the next ask grants), but the buyer sees a generic failure and Google refunds an unacknowledged purchase after three days. A named store error and a log line an admin sees would do.
4. low, defect (read, not run) `prisma/migrations/20261121090000_store_purchases/rollback.sql`: refuses on purchases, store credit packs, store tiers and the product map, but drops `StorePlatformSetting` (an admin's switch and cut) and `StoreNotification` without a check.
5. low, unclear (read, not run) `src/store/store-notification.service.ts` `grantRenewal`: a store renewal of a pass for a member the host has removed writes a new pass period (membership stays pending, so no access) and the person keeps being billed until they cancel; `settle` skips the checks `buy` makes. G-IAP-01-2 covers the removal itself, not the renewal. Lead to rule.
