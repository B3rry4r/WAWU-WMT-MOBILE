A refusal that left no record, or "service not currently available", is a failure and the hold is refunded; a lost answer, a 5xx or any other 2xx is undecided: the payment keeps delivering, Fintava's merchant history is asked, one clear record decides, nothing is bought again, and past PAYMENT_REVIEW_AFTER_HOURS a person looks. The purchase is recorded in the ledger (merchant wallet, out, category `bill`) for MONEY-16.
