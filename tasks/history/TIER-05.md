## Builder report, round 2

**Builder report: TIER-05 round 2 (Sonnet builder, 17 Oct 2026).** Backend `wmt/TIER-05` `f992f4a` on `2f88409` (on batch 6 `fbf391f`); mobile docs only (the contract did not move: `contract:build` twice identical, byte-identical to the mobile `contract/openapi.json`). No migration, no schema change. Nuvion and the Fintava double stood in; no provider or wawuafrica.com host called.

- **1 (high) starvation.** `sweep()` no longer reads the 64 oldest waiting orders. It reads every waiting order a light page at a time (`select` of five columns, keyset on `createdAt, id`), asks the ledger once per page of 256 people for their newest money-in row (`groupBy`, looking back only to the oldest thing a waiting order has seen), and works out who is due with a pure function (`duePeople`, `plan-transfer-order.ts`): a credit newer than the order's last look, or a recheck that came. People a new credit may have paid for are settled first, then rechecks, each oldest first, 4 at a time, until an 18 s budget is spent. A checked order is not due again until a new credit or its recheck, so the next pass takes the rest. Nothing is held across a provider call (the reads finish first). Before: the verifier's D1 left the funded order `awaiting`, 0 sends. After: 70 unfunded then one funded, `paid` 1 on the next sweep and the 70 untouched; 500 unfunded not due and 5 funded with a 1 s provider read, all 5 pay in one sweep and a bystander's `GET /me/tier` stays under 1 s; 500 unfunded due by recheck and 5 funded, the 5 pay and the 500 close in the same pass.
- **2 (low) a made payment expired.** `expireLapsed()` looks for the order's payment under its settlement key before expiring: a payment that is pending or completed is linked (`settled`), never expired. Before: F1 read `expired`, "Nothing was taken", with 1 send. After: `settled`, view `paid`, 1 send.
- **3 (low) the key in a person's space.** The settlement key is now `transfer:<orderId>`. The colon is outside the Idempotency-Key format (8 to 128 letters, digits, - and _), so the guard refuses a client key like it with `400 idempotency_key_required` in the money error shape; nothing in `src/money` changed. Before: A4 linked the order to the other item's payment. After: the order is not linked to it (`paymentId` null).
- **4 (low) a refused payment.** When the wallet payment comes back `failed` (nothing moved), the order is not linked to it: its key is freed, the order returns to `awaiting` with the next check later (1, 2, 4 ... 16 minutes) and the words "Your money arrived, but the payment did not go through and nothing was taken from your wallet. We will try again while this purchase is still open.", or is `expired` if the window has lapsed. The retry is a payment of its own under the same key (the failed one is let go of, so never two payments for one order). The same let-go applies where a stale claim is recovered and where a lapsed order is looked at. Before: B2 left the order `settled`, linked to a failed payment. After: `awaiting`, unlinked, payments `[failed]`; the next try pays once, payments `[failed, completed]`, 1 send; a sweep before the next check makes no second attempt.
