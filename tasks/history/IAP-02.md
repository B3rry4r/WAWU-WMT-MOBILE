**F1 · high · defect (IAP-03): VF13 offers "Add 5 more products" to a person the Hub will refuse, after the store has taken the payment.** The Hub refuses a person with no tier row before it asks the store (`store_purchase_invalid`, 422), but the store sheet has already charged by then. `ProductsFullSheet` draws the offer whenever the store has the product; it never reads the tier state. VF13 opens on any `product_limit_reached`, and that also happens to a person who holds only a verification tick (state `none`, tick cap reached), and for an ended tier plus a tick (the Hub takes the pack but it counts only while a tier runs). Proof (a throwaway test copied from `productsFullStore.test.tsx`, deleted after): `GET /me/tier` state `none`, tier null, 25 of 25 used; the sheet draws the offer at "$4.99" and "Add 5"; pressing it sends the Hub the purchase, the Hub answers the refusal, the store transaction is NOT finished (`fakeStore.finished` is empty), and the red banner shows the Hub's sentence. The Hub's own rule is in `limit-offers.ts`: the pack is offered only with a running tier and when the pack lifts the limit above what is published (`hasTier && withPack > used`), and the 402 carries `extraProducts: null` otherwise; the app ignores both. On Google an unconsumed purchase is paid back after about three days; on Apple nothing refunds it, the person asks. Fix direction: draw the offer only when the tier is `active` or `ending` and `productsAllowed + count > used` (or read the refusal's `extraProducts`), and a Hub refusal after payment should say whether the person was charged.
- The Apple question: the refused consumable stays unfinished and `recover` hands it to the Hub again each time VF13 opens (once per mount). The refusal is counted in `left` and shown nowhere, so there is no loop of errors on screen, only one wasted call per open. **F2 · high · defect (before IAP-03, IAP-01 and IAP-02; not caused by this change): a fan cannot hold passes to two rooms at the same price, and the app then shows a false success.** Passes are one auto-renewing store subscription per price point (`wawu.pass.30`). Google Play lets one account hold a given subscription product once (`ITEM_ALREADY_OWNED`); the App Store lets one Apple ID hold a product once, and one active subscription per subscription group. Trace for a fan with an active pass to room A who buys room B at the same price on Android: the store sheet answers already owned; `purchase()` in `buy.ts` finds the held token with `iap.owned()` and hands it to the Hub with `communityId ?? found.communityId`, which is room B (the room being bought), not room A's; the Hub verifies the token with Google, finds the latest order already granted and answers 201 `alreadyGranted: true` with room A's grant; `PassScreen` shows "You're in" with room A's end date (`buyer.granted`), and the fan has no pass to room B. Nothing is charged for B. Worse, when the latest order is a renewal the Hub has not heard of yet (late Pub/Sub notice), the Hub grants a 30-day period to room B on room A's subscription (my spec: two pass rows A and B, purchase rows O1 for A and O2 for B with the host of B as creator, the subscription holder row still A): room A is not extended and room B's host is credited. `settle` checks the holder's person but not the holder's room. On the App Store the app keeps the room being bought on the phone (`roomIntent.ts`), so a later recovery or Restore hands A's transaction with room B; what StoreKit answers to a second purchase of a held product could not be tried here (no sandbox) and is UNVERIFIED. Groups (IAP-01 setup text, not enforced anywhere): one group for the tiers, one group per pass price point, so a tier and a pass coexist, passes at different prices coexist, and two rooms at one price cannot; if the owner put all passes in one group, buying a second room would be a store plan switch that ends the first. Fix directions for the lead to rule: hand the held token with its own room (`found.communityId` first) and answer "you already hold a pass at this price; cancel it or pick another price" instead of success; make the Hub refuse a different room on a held subscription (`store_purchase_taken`-style, plain sentence); and decide whether room passes should be non-renewing consumable 30-day products (bought per room, repeatable) instead of subscriptions.
