### The host's tools (Hub routes, all new)
`GET /communities/:id/moderation` (`{ muted, mutedMembers }`: the host sees who is muted, a member sees only whether they are), `DELETE /communities/:id/messages/:messageId`, `PUT` and `DELETE /communities/:id/mutes/:userWawuId`. Only the room's host (403 for a member or a stranger). The host cannot mute themselves (400) and only a joined member can be muted (404). A repeat of a mute or an unmute writes nothing new. Tables `CommunityMute` and `CommunityModerationLog` (migration `20261230090000_community_moderation`); every act is logged in the same transaction (who, what, when; a deleted message's id, kind and writer, never its words). A muted send is `403`, `reason.code` `muted_in_room`, "The host has muted you in this room."; nothing is stored or spent; reading is unchanged; a mute survives leaving and rejoining; the host is never muted. Muting reads and writes no pass, credit, refund or wallet.

### The app

Try again." and keeps the sheet). A deleted message leaves the host's screen at once and, round 1, every member's open room at once: the Hub sends `community.message.removed` (the message's id, no words) on the live channel new messages use, to the host and the joined members; the catch-up carries it for a phone that was away (read from the host's log, because the row is gone); the room list and the "my communities" preview read the table, so they lose it with the row (`useRoomMessages` drops it from the arrivals and every page held, and remembers it so a page already on its way cannot bring it back). A member's press and hold still opens the report sheet straight away. A muted member sees one calm line in place of the composer ("The host has muted you in this room. You can still read it."); a send refused as muted removes its bubble and brings the line up. A send refused for a listed word shows the Hub's sentence under the "Not sent" bubble with Edit (the words go back in the field).
