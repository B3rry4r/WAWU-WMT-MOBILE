Findings, ranked:

1. **medium, defect: the data export leaves out the own-voice agreement.** `src/data-export-request/data-export-sections.ts:287-300`, the `voiceProfile` section selects its fields one by one (`consentAt`, `consentLine` for the Voice Intro) and not `ownVoiceConsentAt` and `ownVoiceConsentLine`, the record of what the person agreed to for VoiceOver. `data-export-map.spec.ts` checks models, not columns, so it stays green. Repro: give A the agreement, then run `EXPORT_SECTIONS` `voiceProfile.load` against the stack database: fields are `namePhonetic,nameSavedAt,introText,introTextAt,consentAt,consentLine,sampleAt,audioAt`. Fix: add the two columns (and `savedAt` to the `aiToolJobs` section, `mediaJobId` to `productDrafts`) and one assertion that reads them.
2. **medium, defect: account deletion does not remove a clone the provider still holds.** `src/account-purge/` has no hook for AI tools (compare `src/movies/movies-purge.service.ts`, `AccountPurgeHooks.register`): the purge deletes the `AiToolJob` rows that note a copy (`voice:<id>`), so nothing is left to remove it. Repro on the stack: a clone noted on a job of person B, `AccountPurgeService.purge(B)` (job rows 2 and `VoiceProfile` 1 deleted, so the consent goes), then the stand-in still holds the voice (`clones` 1, `deleted` 0) after 65 seconds and a sweep. The window is narrow (a job in flight, or a provider that refused the delete, at purge time) but the thing left is a person's voice at a third party, after they were told it is taken away. Fix: a `beforeErase` hook that calls `ProviderCopyService.discard` for each `voice:` note of the person and fails (so the sweep tries again) while the provider still holds it, and a spec.
3. **low, defect (sweep list): a clone is forgotten after 24 hours, and a crash leaves one for 15 to 16 minutes.** `provider-copy.service.ts:40` and `:394`, the sweep gives up after `COPY_GIVE_UP_MS` and clears the note, so a provider that refuses the delete for a day keeps the voice with only a log line; for a voice (unlike a transcript) keep trying, or alert. `provider-copy.service.ts:42`, a job killed mid-run stays `working` and its clone is only swept at 15 minutes (`STALE_WORKING_MS`; proved by ageing the job in my own database), and the instant between the provider answering `addVoice` and the Hub noting it (`http-voice-provider.ts:400-407`) leaves an unnoted clone if the Hub dies there. A start-up pass over `working` jobs with a `voice:` note would close the first.
4. **low, defect: `noteCopy` says it noted a copy when the write may have failed.** `ai-job.service.ts:514-518`, `ProviderCopyService.note` never throws (`provider-copy.service.ts:149`) so `noteCopy` returns `true` and the adapter skips its own removal; the in-process discard still runs, so only a crash together with a database failure at that instant leaks.
5. **low, defect: `GET /ai-tools/jobs?saved=false` answers every job, not the unsaved ones.** `ai-job.service.ts:344` handles `true` only; `false` reads as absent. Nothing in the app sends it.
6. **unclear: "Use in a product" attaches any own finished job with audio, saved or not.** `product-draft.service.ts:253` (`assertOwnFinishedJob`). The task's capability check asks only for an own finished result; the brief for this check said "own saved job". Live: an unsaved finished job attached (200). If saved is meant, one extra condition and one assertion.

Test-gap notes (not defects): nothing tests a kill mid-run, the purge or the export for this task (findings 1 to 3 were found only because they were run); an app test for "Not now" on the agreement card would pin that the card's last button is reachable (it sits under the Run bar until the page is scrolled, as in the pictures).
