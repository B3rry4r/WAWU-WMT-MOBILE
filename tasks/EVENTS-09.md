### Defects

- **D1 · defect (medium; a paying holder can be turned away).** The door tells the scanner "The ticket was not checked in." when the Hub did check it in and the answer was lost. Reproduce (`s7_flows.py`, "answer lost"): in the page, let the check-in request through to the Hub and drop its answer (`route.fetch()` then `route.abort()`: the Hub's side effect is real, only the dropped connection is emulated; I first met it by accident, when the web origin was missing from the Hub's CORS list, the browser hid every answer, the screen said "No connection" and the Hub had checked the ticket in). The panel reads "No connection | No connection. Check your internet and try again. The ticket was not checked in." The Hub's row is `checked_in`. "Try again" sends the same code and the answer is "ALREADY USED | Checked in at 20:07 by Door 1", the time a few seconds ago, by the scanner's own door: the person at the door has no way to tell that from a ticket someone else used earlier. A request that was sent and whose answer did not arrive is the usual way a door fails on a venue's mobile data, and `problemOf` (`DoorScreen.tsx`) says "not checked in" for every `network` error, which is true only when the request never left. Expected: say it is not known ("We couldn't reach the Hub. Check the ticket again: if it says already used a moment ago, it went through.") or name E23's own scanner ("by you"). Picture `shots/problem-answer-lost.png`, `problem-answer-lost-retry.png`.
- **D2 · defect (low to medium).** A QR holding more than 500 characters (a long tracking link, a vCard, a poster's calendar entry) is sent whole, the Hub answers 400 "code must be shorter than or equal to 500 characters", and the screen shows "We couldn't check that ticket | code must be shorter than or equal to 500 characters The ticket was not checked in. | Try again | Cancel": the validator's own words as copy, a full stop missing, "not checked in" about something that is not a ticket, and a Try again that sends the same 400. The brief says the door answers every scan with a verdict; the app already answers under 4 characters by padding, and does nothing for the other end. 500 characters exactly is the red verdict (`s6_odd.py`: `len500` 201, `len501` and `len600` 400; picture `shots/odd-len501.png`). Expected: send at most the first 500 characters (the Hub answers `invalid`, a code is never that long) or draw NOT A VALID TICKET itself.
