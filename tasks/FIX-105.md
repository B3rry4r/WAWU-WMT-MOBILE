### How the fan-out is bounded

The request that stores a message writes nothing new (`CommunityMessageService` is unchanged). A sweep (`src/community-message/community-message-notice.service.ts`) runs every 5 s in each Hub instance, takes a lease on one cursor row, reads the messages of the settled window (older than 5 s, at most 10 minutes) in one query, and per room with messages pages the members 500 at a time. Per page it reads read marks, blocks, passes, switches and open notices once, and writes in ONE transaction of at most six statements (a bulk update of the open notices, a bulk insert of the new ones). So a room that posts a hundred messages in a window costs what one costs, and 200 members are one page. Each notice remembers the end of the last window it counted (`countedUpTo`), so a window folded twice counts once. Spec: `community-message-notice.contract.spec.ts` (200 members, three messages: 199 notices, 1 `createMany` of each table, 1 transaction, at most 12 writes in total; the request path calls no notification write).

### Defaults (agent), owner may override

- The phone is buzzed once per notice, when it is first written. Later messages update the notice without a new push until the person opens the room (a new notice then buzzes again).
- With no room named the app opens the Inbox (as `community_join_declined` does), not a Communities chip: the Inbox takes no chip parameter.
- Settings: `COMMUNITY_NOTICE_SETTLE_SECONDS` (5), `COMMUNITY_NOTICE_SWEEP_SECONDS` (5), `COMMUNITY_NOTICE_MAX_WINDOW_SECONDS` (600). A notice shows up about 5 to 10 s after the message.

## Goal

When someone else posts in a community a person joined, they get one notice that counts the new messages, until they open the room. The Z3 switch turns it off. It reaches the phone.

## Scope
