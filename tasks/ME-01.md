### Findings

**D1 · defect · a free piece without a picture has an unreadable view count (M1, light).** On the Content grid, a free piece whose preview is not a photo (video, course, audio, PDF, template: every video in the app, G-290) draws its white 12 caption straight on the light `surfaceSunken` block with no veil: white on #EEECF2, about 1.2 to 1 contrast, so the views figure cannot be read. Paid tiles without a picture get the 0.3 black veil and stay legible; dark mode is legible. Reproduction: a live free piece with `contentType` `pdf` (or `video`), open the Me tab in light mode (`shots/crop-free-nostill-light.png`, the "999" tile in `M1-light-390.png`). G-290 records the kind-of-file tile, not the caption it leaves illegible.

**D2 · defect · a failed refresh replaces a loaded profile with "Your profile did not load" (M1, M10).** `MeScreen` sets `failed = me.isError || fields.isError || stats.isError` without asking whether data is already on screen (the grid does: `query.isError && !query.data`). So a background refetch that fails wipes the whole header, tabs and content. The common path is M10: Save on M8 invalidates `['profile']`, and if that refetch fails the person comes back to "Your profile did not load" instead of their saved profile, and "Profile saved" disappears under it. Reproduction (`tools/bgfail.py`): sign in, Me, Edit profile, change the bio, drop the connection to `GET /users/me/profile-stats` only, Save: at 0 s the toast is over the profile, at 6 s the profile is gone and only the error and Try again remain (`shots/V6-bgfail-0s.png`, `V6-bgfail-6s.png`). The Share button stays enabled over the error.

**D3 · defect · a brand-new account (no profile row) sees "Your content did not load" for ever (M1).** For a signed-in caller the Hub answers `GET /users/{id}/content` with 404 "User not found" when that id has no `UserProfile` row (`content-piece.service.ts` `listByCreator`), and the grid treats that as a failure: "Your content did not load / Something went wrong. Try again." with a no-connection picture, and Try again fails the same way every time. The builder handled the same 404 on `profile-stats` (counts read 0) but not on the content route; jest covers only the stats 404, and the browser case was NOT RUN in the builder report (e). Reproduction: register on WAWU ID (`/auth/register`, confirm the email code), sign in through the app, open Me: counts "0 followers, 0 following, 0 pieces", Content tab shows the error (`shots/V6-new-account-light.png`, `-dark`); `curl` of the content route with that token returns `{"statusCode":404,"message":"User not found"}`. Expected: "Nothing published yet", as for an account with a row and no pieces.
