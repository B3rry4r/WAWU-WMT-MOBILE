### How it works, and what was decided

- The private key is made on the phone (`@noble/curves` P-256, random bytes from `expo-crypto`) and kept in `expo-secure-store` with `requireAuthentication`, so reading it IS the fingerprint or face prompt. A second item, with no prompt, names the account and the Hub's `deviceId`; the key is offered only to the account it was made for.
- Self-healing: the Hub no longer lists this phone (a PIN reset turns it off) or another phone replaced it: the key is dropped and the key is not offered. The system dropped the key (biometrics changed): the key is dropped, the Hub is told to turn it off, the payment falls to the PIN.
- W35's row reads again whenever the wallet read comes in. The row is not drawn on web, on a phone with no biometric enrolled, or where the system will not keep a key behind one (R-47).
- Default (lead), owner may override: turning on also needs one working fingerprint check, so a key that cannot sign is never left on; a closed check turns it off again at the Hub.
- Default (lead), owner may override: the Hub's lock refusal of a passed approval (`423 pin_locked`, G-9) shows the lock sheet, not a "failed" line.

## Goal

A person with a fingerprint or face on their phone turns it on once in Wallet settings and approves payments with it; the PIN is always the way back.

**Finding 1 (high, defect): the chat send can pay twice after a fingerprint that does not approve.** `src/features/chat/ChatSendScreen.tsx:207-229` and the new cases `approval_cancelled` and `approval_failed` call `nothingMoved()`, which drops the intent, sets `key.current = newRequestKey()` and `setKept(null)`. Those two outcomes prove nothing about an earlier attempt: no request left the phone. Repro (scratch test on the chat's own `hubDouble`, mode `lost_after`): the PIN is sent and the answer is lost ("We didn't get an answer", the Hub has paid once), the person taps "Use fingerprint" and closes the prompt, then types the PIN: the second POST carries a NEW Idempotency-Key and the Hub pays again (`PAYMENTS 2`, keys differ). With the same key the Hub would have answered the first payment. The review route is safe (its `nothingMoved` keeps the key). Fix: for the two approval outcomes keep `kept` and the key exactly as the `unanswered` case does (free the key only when the attempt was a first one, `kept?.phase !== 'unknown'`), and add a chat test for it.
