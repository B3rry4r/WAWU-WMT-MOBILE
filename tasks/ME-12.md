## Goal

A person picks a tier (Founding Maker pre-selected, VF5), pays from the wallet or by bank transfer (VF6), and lands on the celebration (VF7) with their badge, points, products to publish and event pass. Hosting events, publishing and uploading all reach this screen when they need a tier.

## Scope

- New files under `src/features/verify/` and route files under `src/app/` (no `_layout.tsx` edit; queue 4 row in SHARED-CHANGES for the route).
- VF5, VF5b: tier cards from `GET /plans` (`tiers`, `preselected`, `priceMinor`, `products`, `bonusPoints`, `days`) and the person's state from `GET /me/tier`; "Upgrade" (VF5b) shows only the difference from `GET /plans/purchases/quote` (`kind` upgrade). VF5a (dollars) is not built at launch (R-16, R-65); a person billed in dollars is not served at launch.
- VF6 and states: `GET /plans/purchases/quote` (tier, optional `bump`: the "Add 1,000 points for ₦800" offer read from `checkoutBump`), then `POST /plans/purchases` (Idempotency-Key, PIN through the pay sheet, MONEY-02) with `method` wallet or transfer; poll `GET /plans/purchases/{id}`. VF6a wallet short opens Add money (W15, WALLET-05; this task adds that tap). VF6b bank transfer details (TIER-05): the person's own account number and the amount still needed. VF6c paying, VF6d payment failed (the Hub's `statusMessage`). G-680 applies to a confirmed payment that cannot be granted.
- VF7, VF7a: confetti, badge, points added, "N products to publish", the event pass, and the button "Record my voice" (opens VF8 when VERIFY-03 is built, else it is not drawn). The Founding number is drawn once TIER-07 serves it (G-421). The confetti colours are #C368EA (maps to `--accent-fg`), #FFB020 and #5C64FF (no token yet: DV2-COMP asks for them).
- This task adds the entries as drawn: "See tiers" on E21 (EVENTS-04) and "Get verified" on the Create hub (M19, ME-04), the tier badge tap on Me, and "Publish" for a person with no tier (VERIFY-04 adds that one).
