**Facts read from the Hub, not from a board.** Roles: at most 15 (`MAX_EXPERIENCE_ROWS`); title, company, location at most 120 and description at most 1000; a month is `YYYY-MM`; an end before the start is refused. Skills: each at most 40 (the contract's), at most 30 on a profile (`SKILLS_MAX_COUNT`, PROVISIONAL on the Hub; the contract states no count, so the Hub's own sentence still says it). Work description: at most 1000 (`WORK_DESCRIPTION_MAX`, PROVISIONAL; the contract's 4000 is only a roomy ceiling). Reorder takes every one of the owner's works or none (a 409 when the list is not exactly theirs), which is why Reorder is offered only on the whole list. ## Goal

A person can write their Experience and Skills on Edit profile and see them on their About tab; a work has a description, can be corrected in place, and the owner can choose the order their works show in.

## Scope
