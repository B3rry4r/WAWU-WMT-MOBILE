Sent to a nearby screen because the kind's own screen is not built or the Hub names nothing to open: sale and tip (Earnings, no sale page), paid_dm_warning and paid_dm_paused (settings, the questions are in the Inbox), credits_low (Inbox rooms), community_join_declined and community_pass_ending without a room (Inbox), community_pass_refunded (history), kyc_verified (Verification), identity_review (Open your wallet), every "no target" fallback above.

`actionHref` is never followed as a path: it is read only against a fixed table (the ten campaign paths, `/communities/<id>`, `/legal/requests/<id>`) and the route built is the app's own. A path outside the table opens nothing.

Open low, for `tasks/SWEEP-APP.md`: (a) the owner's list also names a reply, a comment, a ticket, an event called off, a school or enrolment, a tier, a deletion notice, points and a referral; the Hub writes none of those as notifications today (25 kinds in `NotificationKind`, `PUSH_RULE`). When it does, each gets a row in `routes.ts` and `kinds.ts`; until then a new kind opens the screen its target or category names. (b) The Hub does not resolve a TGIF day into a row's `target` (the contract's target kinds are five), so a TGIF row in the list opens today's reader and its push opens its own day. (c) The "Tap to continue" line under a card is still forward only. Verifier lows (24 Oct 2026, in `history/FIX-102.md`): (d) the sponsored card's back zone sits behind its text rows, so a left tap over the headline may not reach it (re-check live when an ad is booked); (e) on web the Wallet tab needs two browser backs to return to the list; (f) `kinds.ts` copies the Hub's kind list by hand.

## Goal

Tapping a notification opens the screen it is about, in the list and from a push, for every kind the Hub writes. A tap on the left of a TGIF card goes back a card.

## Scope

`wawu-wmt-mobile` only: `src/features/push/routes.ts`, `src/features/notifications/` (`model.ts`, `kinds.ts`, `NotificationFeedScreen.tsx`), `src/features/tgif/` (`TgifReader.tsx`, `model.ts`), `src/features/ads/SponsoredStep.tsx` (one prop: its empty parts pass a touch to the reader's back edge), and their tests.
