## Goal

R-7: 5 uploads and 1 GB free; 25 uploads and 10 GB with a tick. R-8: free pieces are allowed and count toward the
limit; the old "first upload must be free" rule is removed. M11, M24 and M27 show these.

## Scope

Upload and storage allowance from verification state (numbers per R-7); free content per R-8.

Existing items above the new limit stay; only new uploads are refused (V3 shows the web still works). The web allows 2 GB today (OPS-05 report), so 1 GB is lower for existing free accounts.

## Capability checks

- [ ] An unticked creator is stopped at the 6th upload or past 1 GB; a ticked one at the 26th or past 10 GB.
- [ ] A creator can publish a free piece as their first upload or any other, and it counts toward the limit.
- [ ] A creator already above the new limit keeps every existing piece.
