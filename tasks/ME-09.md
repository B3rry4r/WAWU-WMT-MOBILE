## Goal

Creators can edit a piece while it waits, see why it was rejected, send it again, and see sales per piece (M25, M26, M28). Additive only.

## Scope

Edit and resubmit while pending; the latest rejection reason; per-piece sales count.

## Capability checks

- [ ] A rejected piece shows the reviewer's reason and can be resubmitted.
