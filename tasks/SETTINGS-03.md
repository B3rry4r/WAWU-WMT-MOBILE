## Goal

**Defaults (agent), owner may override:**
1. Wrong current password is **400** `CURRENT_PASSWORD_WRONG`, not 401: the app's client treats a 401 as an expired session and tries to refresh; a 400 reaches the form.
2. Limit: **5 wrong current passwords per account per 15 minutes**, the wait also applies to the right password (the same rule the phone codes use). Sign-out: **30 per minute per address**. These are security throttles, not money or legal figures; they are constants in `session-security.service.ts`.
3. "Other devices" means every refresh token of the account; the calling device is handed a fresh pair so it stays signed in (the token has no device identity, G-91).
4. Sign-out needs no access token (the access token may be expired) and proves itself by the refresh token; it answers 200 for any token (no enumeration, and a signed-out device never sees an error).
5. New password rule is the reset rule (8 to 128 characters), no extra complexity rule.
6. Setting the same password as now is refused (`PASSWORD_UNCHANGED`).
7. No "your password was changed" email: the account's email may be unproven (G-27) and no copy exists; a mail is a later add-on, not a launch need.
8. Other devices' access tokens are not cut off early (G-90); 15 minutes is accepted.
