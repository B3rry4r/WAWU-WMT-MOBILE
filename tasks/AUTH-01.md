### Defaults decided at hand-over

Default (agent), owner may override:

- **F2, no mail to an unproven address.** wawu-id sends no reset mail to an email that was never proved (an app sign-up's). A link sent to an address someone typed at sign-up but never proved would let a stranger who typed a victim's address take over the account, which is why wawu-id withholds it. The fix is in AUTH-05 (prove the email in the sign-up sequence) or a reset by a code to the proven phone (G-27). A24 is not made to behave differently when no mail went out: the API answers identically for a known, an unknown and an unproven address by design, so the app cannot know.
- **F3, F4, F12** are filed, not coded: G-29 (timing difference and the phone-only 500 on forgot-password, wawu-id, no task yet), G-28 (no limit on login or forgot-password; OPS-11 does NOT cover it, its scope is the Hub's Flutterwave webhook throttle and `trust proxy`; settle before the store release), G-30 (the reset mail and web page are WAWU-branded while the app is "Who Made This", R-37; web and wawu-id owner).
