**Findings.**

1. **defect (D1): the message limits overshoot under parallel sends.** `checkLimits` counts, and the message is written later, with no lock. Repro (`p5.js`, stand-in delay 150 ms): 60 parallel `POST /legal/assistant/{id}/messages` on one fresh conversation: 60 accepted, 60 stored, 60 AI calls, against a limit of 30 an hour. 36 old messages plus 20 parallel: 52 stored, limit 40 per conversation. 25 recent on an earlier intake plus 20 parallel: 44 in the hour, limit 30. Sequential sends stop at exactly 30 (429, `retryAfterSeconds` 3599). The limits exist to cap paid AI calls, so a client can multiply them. The task asked for no overshoot.
2. **defect (D2): `POST {id}/reply` is unlimited and not serialized.** Fifteen parallel replies against one waiting client message: 9 AI calls, 9 assistant messages written, 6 x 409. It is also outside the 30 an hour count, so repeating it while the provider fails costs a paid call each time.

**How the limits are now enforced (D1, D2, D7).** One rule for everything that spends a person's allowance: the check and the write that spends it are ONE transaction under a per-person Postgres advisory lock (`pg_advisory_xact_lock(hashtextextended('legal-assistant:' || sub, 0))`, the same pattern as `beneficiary.service.ts`), and the provider is called after the transaction, never inside it. What counts toward the 30 an hour per person: a client message before Send (`LegalIntakeMessage`), a reply asked for again and a brief prepared on Send (new table `LegalAssistantCall`, `kind` `reply` or `brief`, no words in it), and a client message after Send (`LegalChatMessage` of the person's own matters, joined through `LegalRequest.wawuUserId`). What counts toward the 40 per conversation: client messages plus the calls of that intake (checked for a message and a reply, not for the brief, because "conversation full" tells the client to send the brief). Two things the first version did not say: the brief has its own cap (5 tries an hour, `ASSISTANT_BRIEF_ATTEMPTS_PER_HOUR`, `PROVISIONAL(LEGAL-ASSISTANT-BRIEF-ATTEMPTS-PER-HOUR, owner=YOU, why=...)`, also counted in the 30), and a refusal writes nothing and calls nothing. A tap on a quick reply is resolved in the same step, so a double tap is one message and the second is `400 quick_reply_unknown`.
