## Goal

**Findings.**

1. **defect (D1): the message limits overshoot under parallel sends.** `checkLimits` counts, and the message is written later, with no lock. Repro (`p5.js`, stand-in delay 150 ms): 60 parallel `POST /legal/assistant/{id}/messages` on one fresh conversation: 60 accepted, 60 stored, 60 AI calls, against a limit of 30 an hour. 36 old messages plus 20 parallel: 52 stored, limit 40 per conversation. 25 recent on an earlier intake plus 20 parallel: 44 in the hour, limit 30. Sequential sends stop at exactly 30 (429, `retryAfterSeconds` 3599). The limits exist to cap paid AI calls, so a client can multiply them. The task asked for no overshoot.
2. **defect (D2): `POST {id}/reply` is unlimited and not serialized.** Fifteen parallel replies against one waiting client message: 9 AI calls, 9 assistant messages written, 6 x 409. It is also outside the 30 an hour count, so repeating it while the provider fails costs a paid call each time.
3. **defect (D3, low): Send calls the AI before it claims the intake.** 20 parallel sends open one matter (correct) but `generateBrief` ran 19 times; a failing brief can be re-sent without limit. Claim first, or serialize per intake.
4. **defect (D4, low): a NUL character in `body` is a 500.** A body of `a`, U+0000, `b` returns 500 "Something went wrong" (Postgres rejects 0x00); nothing is saved and nothing of the text is logged, but a user input must be a 400. Refuse or strip it in the DTO.
5. **defect (D5, low): opening is not idempotent under concurrency.** 12 parallel `POST /legal/assistant` for one person created 12 `in_progress` intakes (distinct ids, no unique index); 11 are orphans with two scripted rows each, and "pick up the unfinished one" is then arbitrary. A double mount of the screen can do this.
6. **defect (D6, low): the paid-wait assistant's replies keep em-dashes.** Only profiling turns pass `parseTurn`. After payment (status `consultation_scheduled`) a stand-in reply containing an em-dash comes back to the client unchanged in the assistant thread (`ppaid.js`). `LegalChatService` is existing code, but this feature is what now shows its text on S17, and the product rule is no em-dashes in anything a user reads. Default (agent) 6 says AI text loses em-dashes; it does not hold for this path.
7. **defect (D7, low): nothing limits messages after Send.** 60 more messages on a sent, paid conversation: 60 accepted and 61 AI calls in all (each paid-wait message is an AI call); before payment each is an unbounded database row (up to 2000 characters). The 30 an hour and 40 limits cover only the profiling stage.
8. **unclear (U1): migration order and a rival branch.** `20261006210000_legal_assistant` sorts after main (up to `20261006100000`) and after every open branch except `wmt/INBOX-07` (`20261007090000_inbox_feed_index`), so SHARED-CHANGES LEGAL-01 #1's claim that it "sorts after every `wmt/*` branch" is not true today; the two are independent, so it matters only if the rule is applied strictly. Separately `origin/wmt/LEGAL-01-b` (adf26db, "the legal chat starts before anyone pays") creates the same table `LegalIntakeMessage` and the same column `LegalIntake.channel` in `20261006120000_legal_assistant_chat`, with files in `src/legal-intake/assistant/`: only one of the two can merge.
9. **differs-for-recorded-reason (G-202):** a deleted account's `LegalChatMessage` rows (the consultant's and the client's pre-payment messages) remain after purge; BACKEND_GAPS G-202, owner SETTINGS-06.
10. **note (web):** `GET /legal/intake/mine` now also lists an assistant conversation (`matter other`, `in_progress`) for the web, and the form's `PATCH answers` accepts the owner's assistant intake id; neither harms the web's resume. The form routes answer 403 for someone else's assistant intake and 404 for a missing one (existing `findOwned` behaviour, uuid ids).
11. **note (contract):** the contract lists only success responses; the `reason.code` values are in controller comments only. Mobile `contract/openapi.json` is byte-identical to the backend branch's.
