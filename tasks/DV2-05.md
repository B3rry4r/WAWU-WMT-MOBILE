## Goal

Sign-up asks for email, phone and the terms line only; the selfie is a hand-off to Nuvion's secure page; the wallet-open result is "Approved" with the account number; the BVN mismatch screen is entered from "Complete your account".

## Scope

- Rework of AUTH-04 and AUTH-07 (A3, A4, A15) and KYC-03, KYC-04, KYC-05 (A6, A7, A8, A14), `src/features/auth/` and `src/features/open-wallet/`. SHARED-04's native selfie (A6, A8 cited) is replaced by the hosted page.
- A3: email, phone and the line "I agree to the terms and privacy policy" (R-67, shown now); no BVN field. A4, A15: copy no longer says "the number on your BVN". A6: "You'll finish this on a secure page" hands off to Nuvion's hosted selfie (`GET` and `POST /money/identity/liveness`); A7 "Back from the secure page" on return. A8: "Approved" with the account number; the bank name comes from the Hub, the licence line stays hidden (R-67). A14 is entered from A31.
- Whether Nuvion can start the hosted selfie for our users is R-39's fact; `IdentityLivenessView.enabled` says so. If it is false, the selfie step is dropped as R-39 says, and the report says which.
