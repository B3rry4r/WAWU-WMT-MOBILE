## Goal

The profile header on Me matches the 16 Oct boards: the tier badge, the products meter, the Draft tag on draft products and the Voice Intro player when there is one, and the "Get your professional tick" card is gone.

## Scope

- Rework of ME-01 (`src/features/me/`: `MeScreen.tsx`, `ProfileHeader.tsx`, `model.ts`). New code only; the tick card comes out in DV2-RM.
- Tier badge and "N of M products" meter for the owner from `GET /me/tier` (`TierBadge`, `ProductsMeter` from DV2-COMP). The Draft tag on draft products from `GET /product-drafts`. The Voice Intro player ("Bio" with the "AI voice" tag, R-66) appears once the Hub serves `voiceIntro` (VOICE-01, G-800); until then it is not drawn.
- M3 (Shop tab) stays not built (R-9). Brand copy "Who Made This?" (R-69).
