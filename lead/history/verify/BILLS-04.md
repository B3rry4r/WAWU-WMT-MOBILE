## Findings
1. MEDIUM. A lost purchase is matched to the PREVIOUS bill's record in Fintava history and shown "delivered" with the previous bill's token.
   src/wallet-bills/wallet-bill-rules.ts:221-262 (HISTORY_SKEW_MS = 2 min, `readHistory`), used by wallet-bill.service.ts:737-775 (`reconcile`).
   readHistory accepts any history row made up to 2 minutes BEFORE the purchase was sent, at the same amount and meter (or phone/smartcard tail), and never excludes a row already stored on another bill (`fintavaTransactionId`).
   Repro (zz-vattack5 "second-bill-after-504", zz-vattack2 "history match"): pay electricity bill A (₦10,000, meter M) and let it deliver; its record is in merchant history; create and pay bill B (same meter, same amount) within 2 minutes while Fintava answers the purchase 502/504 (nothing bought); one sweep. B becomes `delivered` with A's token, the hold is RELEASED, only one purchase exists at Fintava. The person paid twice and received one token, silently. For airtime, data and cable the "delivered" has no token at all, so nothing shows the loss.
   Fix: in `reconcile`, drop history rows whose id or reference is already on another WalletBill; also shorten the skew or require the row to be after `purchaseSentAt` minus a few seconds. Add a spec with two same-account same-amount bills.
2. LOW. Daily bill limit counts a refunded bill. zz-vattack2 "daily bill limit vs refunded bill": limit = one bill; first bill fails and is refunded; the next bill's quote says `withinDailyLimit:false` and pay is `403 limit_reached`. Cause: money-limits.service.ts `movedSince` sums the user's completed `out` ledger rows and does not net the refund `in` row. Shared WALLET-23 code, same for every held payment, not introduced by this task; but biller failures make it likelier for bills (cable answers "service not available" for every attempt).
3. LOW. Under WALLET_PROVIDER=nuvion only airtime answers `409 target_not_payable` on create. Electricity, data and cable answer `503 bills_unavailable` when Fintava has no key (zz-vattack3), because `price()` (wallet-bill.service.ts:174-208, 266-280, 390-405) asks the Fintava lists before the `lines()` provider check. With a Fintava key still set, create would call Fintava (disco list, meter check, bundle or plan list) first and then answer 409. Quote, pay, generic `POST /money/payments` kind bill and a draft made before the switch all answer 409 `target_not_payable`, nothing moves (read of an own draft stays 200). Brief expected 409 everywhere: check the provider before `price()`.
4. LOW. WAWU's bill fee is in no `wawuFeeKobo`. With WAWU_FEE_BILL_KOBO=5000 and a ₦120 bill charge the quote, the debit (₦10,185.75) and `GET /money/fees/quote?kind=bill` agree, but the ledger rows (user out, merchant in, merchant bill out) and the payment row all carry wawuFee 0; the user's row reads amount ₦10,170 + fee ₦15.75. Fee reports built from the ledger (MONEY-16, the dashboard) will miss WAWU's bill revenue. The bill view itself is right (serviceFeeKobo 5000).
5. LOW. A failed bill says "Your money was returned to your wallet." (BILL_FAILED_REASON, wallet-bill-rules.ts:80) at once, but the refund is a separate move: it was ₦15.75 short (Fintava's charge on the first transfer, G-462, recorded) and with a frozen payer it waits for the hold sweep (`returned.returned:false` is right, the copy is not). Re-paying a failed bill says "This bill has already been paid" (BILL_ALREADY_PAID_MESSAGE).
6. INFO (by design). A purchase whose answer is lost and which has no record in merchant history stays `processing` with the hold HELD (32 simulated hours: no re-buy, 1 purchase, no review yet); a record that shows up later settles it as delivered. After PAYMENT_REVIEW_AFTER_HOURS a person looks. A "refusal after debit" (Fintava debits the merchant, then answers 400) is refunded to the person and WAWU is out the amount (system delta -₦10,000); a success after a refund is not picked up. These rest on "a 4xx means nothing happened" (G-647 guesses).

Cost otherwise small: 2 queries plus at most 1 Fintava read per run, nothing while no unnoted failed bill is under 2 days old. It only reads: never re-buys, never moves money.

If only one was bought (T4) the other waits held with the single purchase delivered once; a person refunds it after the review time.
